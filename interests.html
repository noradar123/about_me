01 / Olympiads

Physics, linguistics, and geography

I took part in Taiwan's national physics, linguistics, and geography Olympiads and did well across these subjects. Preparing for them gave me a reason to spend time on topics outside my usual coursework and work through unfamiliar problems.

I enjoy the different kinds of thinking each subject calls for: understanding a physical system, finding patterns in a language, or connecting what I know about a place with the clues in front of me. That curiosity is still a big part of how I learn.
