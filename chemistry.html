Study, research, and IChO

Chemistry.

Chemistry was a big part of my life before I started studying computer science and mathematics. Here are some of the things I worked on, from studying reactions and doing experiments to research and the International Chemistry Olympiad.

Study & experiments ↓Research ↓IChO 2023 ↓

01 / Chemistry

Before IChO

I used to be a big chemistry nerd. I spent a lot of my time studying chemistry, working through problems, and doing experiments. I enjoyed both sides of it: sitting down with a reaction mechanism and trying to understand each step, then spending time in the lab working with actual materials.

What kept me interested was the connection between the theory and what I could observe. A structure drawn on a blackboard and a flask on a lab bench were two ways of looking at the same subject. I liked moving between them and getting a better understanding of how things worked.

That interest led me to spend more time on both chemistry research and competition preparation.

02 / Research

Metal clusters & ATRP polymers

I also worked on research involving metal clusters and polymers made through atom transfer radical polymerization (ATRP). These projects gave me a chance to explore chemistry beyond coursework and competition problems, and to spend more time connecting what I was learning with experimental work.

I liked the combination of understanding the underlying chemistry and working through practical questions in the lab. That mix of theory and hands-on work is still a big part of what interests me. Today, I spend more of my time on computer science and mathematics, but I still enjoy taking an idea apart, understanding the details, and trying it out for myself.

03 / IChO 2023

International Chemistry Olympiad

In Grade 11, I represented Chinese Taipei at the 2023 International Chemistry Olympiad in Switzerland. I won a gold medal, placing 12th out of 348 contestants.

One of the best parts was meeting talented chemistry students from all over the world. We shared an interest that had taken up a large part of our lives, even though we came from very different places. I got to know a lot of great people, and those encounters are as much a part of how I remember IChO as the competition itself.
